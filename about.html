WHAT IS STANDARDCORE?

What is StandardCore?

StandardCore is an internet-born aesthetic centered on ordinary objects that became standards of everyday life.

These are not necessarily masterpieces, luxury goods, or objects designed to attract attention. They are things we see in supermarkets, offices, schools, hospitals, workshops, kitchens, and convenience stores — objects repeated so often that they become part of the background.

A pen used by millions.
A bottle whose shape has remained familiar for decades.
A watch made in enormous numbers.
A package recognized before its name is read.
A tool whose design became so ordinary that we stopped noticing it.

StandardCore looks at these objects not simply as commodities, but as material culture.

It is interested in repetition, familiarity, longevity, mass production, functional design, and the strange anonymity that comes from being everywhere.

Some StandardCore objects are celebrated design classics. Others have no such status. What connects them is not prestige, but persistence.

They remain.
They are repeated.
They become familiar.
Eventually, they become invisible.

NOT ABOUT PRESTIGE

StandardCore is not a collection of the best products.

It is not about luxury, rarity, price, trend, or status.

An object can be inexpensive, anonymous, disposable, industrial, or almost completely unnoticed — and still become part of the shared visual language of everyday life.

AN OPEN AESTHETIC

StandardCore is not limited to the objects in this archive.

Every country, city, workplace, school, household, and generation has its own standards.

The archive is only a starting point.

If an ordinary object feels so familiar that nobody thinks to document it, it may be worth looking at again.
